# 🦄 Empire of Sugar 🌈

_You start with one unicorn, a bathtub in the corner, and no idea what is out there._

A turn-based game of light and sugar on a board hidden under clouds.

- **Explained in-game.** Jump right in; come back for the hints below if you get stuck.
- **Seven boards**, from a three-turn tutorial to 25×25.
- **From the fifth board on, a rival**: a dark unicorn from the opposite corner.

## 🤖 On the use of AI

A deliberate split of roles:

- **Mine**: every design decision (ideas, theme, mechanics, economy, balancing) and the template from my 2025 entry, size tricks included.
- **Claude Code's**: the implementation, byte-golfing, and the bot I balanced against.

Open questions came to me, not the AI.

## ⚠️ Spoilers below

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mxlle/13k-2026-unicorns-and-rainbows/main/spoilers-tiles.png)

- **Balance the two incomes. This matters most.** 💧 pays for each step, 🍬 buys unicorns. Both come from your rainbows: one beside a 🍭 makes 🍬, otherwise 💧. Water without unicorns is wasted; unicorns without water cannot move.
- **Exploring is a multiplier.** Every 🌈 and 🦄 scores 1 point per % of ☁️ cleared.
- **The middle tile is always a 🚿.** A second bathtub is more income and another place to buy unicorns. With a rival, only one of you gets it.
- **A new unicorn pays for itself.** More unicorns clear more clouds and light more fountains, so buy early.
- **Unicorns get more expensive**: each new one costs one 🍬 per unicorn you have.
- **Let them grow.** Three turns of shining earn a rank, up to 3; a rank 3 rainbow pays three times as much.
- **One fountain holds several rainbows**, one per free side. Cheaper than a new fountain.
- **Save steps.** Steps off a 🍮 are free. 🍩 are portal pairs: jump for 2 💧.
- **Presents can wait.** Their color shows what is inside. Take 💧 early, 🍬 and unicorns once you can afford to move a whole herd.
